    How this workspace earns trust

    • Propose, never act — the engine refuses any transition that is not an explicit human decision.
    • Provenance on every block — model id, model-reported uncertainty, and finish reason travel with the text.
    • Risk flags, honestly labeled — heuristic bias patterns are surfaced but never presented as evaluation.
    • Veto is first-class — rejecting requires a reason, and the reason is part of the audit log.
    • Nothing stored server-side — threads are in-memory only; the log lives with you and is yours to export.
