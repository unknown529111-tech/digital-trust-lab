    How VeriLoop earns trust

    • Evidence-in-the-loop, enforced in code — the engine refuses any human decision on an unverified suggestion; no approval before evidence review.
    • Claims, not vibes — every answer is decomposed into verifiable claims; each claim gets a verdict (supported / partial / unsupported / contradicted).
    • Cross-checked, not self-graded — live mode verifies with a different model than the generator, so agreement is not baked in.
    • Contradictions are loud — conflicting sources are flagged visually and in the audit log; verification can fail while the model was confident.
    • Propose, never act — the AI cannot act on its own, and the human judges after seeing the evidence, not before.
    • Models under test — the dashboard accumulates per-model claim survival rates (NIST TEVV; EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency).
    • Nothing stored server-side — threads are in-memory only; the log lives with you and is yours to export.
